Quick answers, iOS

First transcription needs a one-time download of an accuracy option in Settings; after that everything works offline, including Airplane Mode. If recording seems silent, check that Vertano has microphone access in iOS Settings. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple; restore a purchase any time from Settings inside the app.

Quick answers, desktop

The desktop apps are open source; guides and downloads live on thedownload page, and issues are welcome on GitHub.
