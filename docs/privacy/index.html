Privacy

The whole policy fits on one page.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Your recordings and transcripts

Vertano processes audio, captions, and translations entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded, logged, or seen by us. There are no accounts and no analytics inside the apps.

Language packs and models

Speech models download once from public model repositories when you choose them. On-device translation may download language packs through your operating system the first time you use a language. These downloads fetch files to your machine; they carry nothing back about you or your content.

The iOS app

Vertano for iPhone and iPad works the same way: recording, transcription, and translation all happen on your device, and your recordings and transcripts stay there. The app asks for microphone access only so it can record; declining still lets you import and transcribe files. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Purchases

Optional upgrades in the iOS app are bought through Apple with your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

This website

vertano.app is a static site. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

Questions

The desktop apps are open source; for them, the surest answer is the source itself, at github.com/fahdi/stenodrop.
